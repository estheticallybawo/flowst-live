Assumptions

Core Growth Assumptions

  • The 10% School Conversion Moat: 50 active paying students per partnered campus out of a 500-student body via Phase 2 competitions.
  • Wider Digital Adoption in Nigeria: Rising trust in online-first education, as seen in Miva Open University's growth, supports faster onboarding and sustained engagement for digital learning products.
  • Retention Stability: 6-month minimum subscription built into competition eligibility rules.
  • Founder-led Sales: Execution across 6 target states keeps Year 1 acquisition minimal
  • State Representative Network: Leverage local educators and administrators to drive adoption and provide feedback.
Why this works

Competition-driven engagement creates a durable acquisition loop that converts school excitement into parent-paid student subscriptions while using FOMO as a moat to prepare for future growth.

A software-first cost base and simple per-user COGS keep margins healthy as scale grows.

15
Phygital Go-to-Market Approach

Validate small, build proof, then scale distribution.

Phase 1 - Classroom Pedagogy Pilot (Targeting September, pending school outreach)

Partner with a single classroom and teacher to test how Flowst's agents support real learning over a 30-60 day period, using whatever screen infrastructure the school already has. The goal is not scale; it is validating that the pedagogy actually works before building anything bigger on top of it.

Phase 2 - B2C Curiosity Campaign

Using data and insight from Phase 1, launch a broader consumer-facing social campaign and student competition: build or create something from curiosity, document the process, and present how it works. This drives awareness, community, and a wave of students who want Flowst in their school.

Phase 3 - B2B Classroom Integration

With validated pedagogy and organic student demand as leverage, pitch schools on Flowst as a teacher's classroom assistant, at $799/month, including hardware support where needed, subject to funding secured for hardware deployment.

Phase 4 & 5 - Recognition & Expansion

Announce competition winners, formalize additional school partnerships, and expand into more classrooms and states.
